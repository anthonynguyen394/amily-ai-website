## Is Amily compliant with Victorian and federal regulations?

Yes. Compliance is the reason we built Amily in Melbourne for Australian businesses, rather than buying a US AI receptionist and rebranding it. The four compliance pillars:

- **Privacy Act 1988 and APP 5** — Amily identifies itself as an AI and notifies the caller of recording at the start of every call (APP 5 notification, not buried in a privacy policy).
- **APP 8 (cross-border disclosure)** — recordings and transcripts are stored in Australian-hosted infrastructure, per-tenant isolated, never used to train a general model.
- **APP 11 (data security)** — recordings and transcripts are encrypted in transit and at rest, and you can request export or deletion at any time.
- **Surveillance Devices Act 1999 (Vic)** — your business is a party to calls to its own number, so recording is lawful with disclosure under section 6. Section 11 restricts downstream sharing, and Amily's architecture prevents that by default.
- **Privacy and Other Legislation Amendment Act 2024** — the ADM (automated decision-making) transparency rules apply from **10 December 2026**. Amily ships ready-to-use privacy policy copy covering booking, triage and qualification decisions, so your policy is compliant on day one.

The full architecture is documented in our [privacy policy](/privacy), and we provide every customer with aligned privacy policy copy for their own site.
