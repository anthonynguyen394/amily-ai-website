## How much does a missed call really cost a Melbourne plumber or electrician?

A single missed call to a working tradie is worth between **AUD $200 and AUD $800** in lost job value, depending on the type of work. That is the headline number. The job does not always go to voicemail — it usually goes to the next tradie on Google who picks up.

The total annual cost depends on call volume, miss rate, and average job value. Industry research puts the average annual cost at about **$52,000** for a solo plumber or electrician in a metro like Melbourne, which is roughly a quarter of small-trade revenue [Source: industry research benchmark, 2025]. The number lands in a band that feels right to most tradies who have done their own rough math: if you take 25 calls a week and miss a third of them, you are watching several jobs a week go to a competitor.

The harder cost is the one you do not see: the customer who would have become a repeat client, the referral they would have sent, the five-star Google review they would have left. One missed call today is often a missed customer for years.

## How many calls do Melbourne tradies actually miss each week?

Most solo Melbourne tradies receive **15-30 calls a week**. During work hours, 20-30% of those go to voicemail because the tradie is up a ladder or under a house with their phone on silent. After hours — weeknights, weekends, public holidays — 100% of calls go unanswered.

In raw numbers, that is 6-10 missed calls a week, or **300-500 missed calls a year**. Not all of those callers were ready to book, but tradies consistently report that 25-35% of missed calls would have turned into paid work. Run the math: 350 missed calls × 30% would-have-booked = 105 jobs a year, at an average value of $500, and you are at $52,500 in lost revenue.

The after-hours bucket is the worst. A burst pipe at 9pm on a Sunday is exactly the customer who pays a callout premium and remembers you for the next ten years. That is the call you cannot afford to miss, and it is the one you are missing right now.
